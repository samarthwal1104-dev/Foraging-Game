A Serious-Game Paradigm to Evaluate Human Perceptual and Behavioural Responses to Drone AcousticsAuthor: Samarth Wal (21AE30022)Institution: Department of Aerospace Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology KharagpurSupervisors: Prof. Sandeep Saha (IIT Kharagpur), Prof. Cade McCall (University of York)Thesis Submission: May 2026 (Project-II, AE57004)Engine Version: Unity 6000.0.55f1OverviewThis repository contains the Unity source project for a 3D first-person foraging serious game developed to investigate how drone acoustics modulate human perceptual and behavioural responses under simulated threat.Participants forage for clustered resources (Red Apples = 10 pts, Golden Apples = 50 pts) across a 120-second trial in a fantasy forest environment while being tracked by an autonomous aerial drone hovering at a 10 m altitude. To isolate the effect of drone acoustics on risk-taking and explore–exploit trade-offs, two conditions are compared while keeping visual drone behaviour identical:High-Noise Condition: Full-volume continuous propeller audio loop.Low-Noise Condition: Identical propeller audio passed through a 5000 Hz low-pass filter.When the drone locks on and approaches, players incur proximity score penalties unless they adopt defensive hiding behaviour (crouching).Demo VideoBecause the full-resolution gameplay demonstration video is 142 MB, it is hosted via GitHub Releases rather than tracked directly in the Git commit history:Watch / Download Demo Video: View on the Releases PageKey Experimental Findings ($N = 31$)Cognitive Risk Recalibration: Under the High-Noise condition, participants initiated crouching earlier ($p = 0.0521$), waited until the drone retreated further before standing ($p = 0.0448$), and maintained a significantly larger clearance distance from the drone when collecting apples ($\beta = +6.132, p = 0.0143$).Motor & Spatial Stability: Elevated drone noise did not impair physical navigation or motor execution—transit time, transit distance, obstacle collisions, and overall trajectory density remained invariant across conditions.Temporal Habituation: Threat detection distances converged toward baseline during the second 60-second half of the trial, demonstrating rapid auditory habituation.Repository StructureOnly essential Unity source folders are tracked in this repository (auto-generated cache folders such as Library/, Temp/, Logs/, and UserSettings/ are excluded via .gitignore):Assets/ — Custom C# scripts (player controller, drone AI & acoustic filtering, dynamic patch spawning, automated telemetry loggers), scenes, prefabs, and environment assets.Packages/ — Unity Package Manager manifest (manifest.json and packages-lock.json).ProjectSettings/ — Input, audio, physics, and quality configurations.Data Logging OutputsAt the end of each
